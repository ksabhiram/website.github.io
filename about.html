Philosophy

Built for Science,
Designed for Humanity.

I am a physicist and materials science researcher. My work focuses on thermoelectricity — the physics of converting heat directly into electrical energy through quantum and classical transport phenomena.

Having completed my academic training at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune, my research explores how atomic-scale point defect engineering, chemical doping, and grain-boundary scattering can suppress lattice thermal conductivity while sustaining robust power factors.

Science is too important to remain locked behind opaque jargon. Alongside laboratory investigations, I write pedagogical reviews, essays, and course notes designed to respect the reader's intellect and time.

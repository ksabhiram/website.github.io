● In Preparation · 2026
Comprehensive Review on PEDOT:PSS / Bi-Te Based Organic–Inorganic Thermoelectric Composites and Flexible Device Architectures
Kurapati S. Abhiram, Chia-Ji Liu, et al.
Manuscript in Preparation · Novel Materials and Thermoelectrics Laboratory, NCUE, Taiwan
A comprehensive state-of-the-art review exploring the interface transport physics, energy filtering, and power factor enhancement mechanisms in Bismuth–Tellurium chalcogenides blended with conducting polymer PEDOT:PSS. Evaluates room-temperature solution synthesis pathways, chemical stability, and device engineering trade-offs for wearable and flexible thermoelectric generators.
PEDOT:PSS Bi-Te Flexible Thermoelectrics Review
In Preparation Preprint Soon
● In Preparation · 2026
Band Engineering and Defect Chemistry in Cu-Sb-Se-Te Chalcogenide Composites for High-Efficiency Thermoelectric Power Generation
Kurapati S. Abhiram, Suresh Perumal, et al.
Manuscript in Preparation · Laboratory for Energy and Advanced Devices (LEAD), IIT Hyderabad
Investigation into Cu-Sb-Se based chalcogenides synthesised via solid-state reaction and densified through induction/resistive hot pressing. Te alloying is systematically leveraged to tune carrier concentration, promote multi-valley band convergence, and introduce nanoscale mass-fluctuation phonon scattering for elevated thermoelectric figure of merit (zT).
Cu-Sb-Se Te Alloying Band Engineering zT Optimisation Hot Pressing
In Preparation Preprint Soon

My favourite author for all foundational physics courses. The legend, the boon for all undergrads of India! Covering Elements of Non-Equilibrium Statistical Mechanics, Mathematical Physics: Selected Topics, and classical dynamics.
